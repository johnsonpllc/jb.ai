01This Week's Tip

Main Tip: Ditch the Discovery Call for Smart Intake

Stop trading 15 billable minutes to answer questions your intake form could handle automatically.

  • Replace your discovery call with a conditional intake form (Typeform or Jotform work well) that branches based on client type, matter complexity, or service needed.
  • Use an AI tool like ChatGPT or Claude to analyze submitted responses and draft a personalized follow-up email before you ever read the form yourself.
  • Flag high-value or complex matters automatically using keyword triggers, so you know exactly which prospects actually need a live conversation.
  • According to Clio's 2023 Legal Trends Report, clients expect faster responses — automated follow-up within minutes sets you apart immediately.
  • Reserve live calls only for engagements above a set revenue threshold or complexity level you define.
