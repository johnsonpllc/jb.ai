This Week's Tip

Stop scheduling discovery calls for information you could collect before the first "hello."

  • Build a smart intake form using Typeform or Notion — ask about matter type, urgency, budget range, and prior counsel upfront.
  • Let AI do the follow-up triage. Tools like Zapier + ChatGPT can auto-summarize responses and flag high-value or complex leads instantly.
  • Pre-qualify without the back-and-forth. According to Clio's 2023 Legal Trends Report, 59% of clients expect a response within a few hours — a fast, automated acknowledgment buys you that time.
  • Route by matter type automatically. Send tax clients one workflow, litigation clients another — no manual sorting.
  • Reserve your live time for billable work, not logistics. Your intake form should do the qualifying; you show up ready to advise.
