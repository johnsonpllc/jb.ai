This Week's Tip

Stop letting your AI sound like everyone else's AI — here's how to fix it fast.

  • Paragraph 1 — Role & Voice: Tell the AI *who* it is. Example: "You are a no-fluff business coach who speaks directly and uses short sentences."
  • Paragraph 2 — Your Rules: List what it should *never* do. "Avoid corporate jargon, don't use bullet points unless asked, skip the filler phrases like 'certainly!'"
  • Paragraph 3 — Your Audience: Describe exactly who it's talking to. The more specific, the sharper the output.
  • Use real examples from your own writing — paste 2-3 sentences you've written and say "match this tone."
  • Test it immediately by asking it something you'd normally answer yourself, then tweak until it feels *right.*
